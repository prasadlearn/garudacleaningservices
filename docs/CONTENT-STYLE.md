# Garuda Cleaning Services — Plain English Content Style Guide

This guide is the standing standard for all website copy across all pages.

---

## 1. Core Principles

1. **Short, Clear Sentences**
   - Keep sentences brief and direct (aim for 10–18 words).
   - Express one clear idea per sentence.
   - Use common, everyday words instead of formal or corporate vocabulary.

2. **No Stacked Marketing Adjectives**
   - ❌ Avoid: *"Comprehensive, advanced, mechanized, premium, trusted deep cleaning solutions."*
   - ✅ Use: *"We clean homes, flats, and offices deeply using modern scrubbers and vacuums."*

3. **Plain Equipment & Method Descriptions**
   - Equipment and method names (e.g. *rotary floor scrubbers*, *vacuum extractors*, *acid-free descalers*) are good to keep, but must sit inside natural, understandable sentences.
   - ❌ Avoid: *"Mechanized Rotary Floor Scrubbing and Micro-Extraction."*
   - ✅ Use: *"We use rotary floor scrubbers for deep floor cleaning."*

4. **No Business Jargon or Corporate Buzzwords**
   - ❌ *"Doorstep cleaning crew"* ➔ ✅ *"Our team comes to your home"*
   - ❌ *"Joint room-by-room handover audit"* ➔ ✅ *"We check every room together with you before we finish"*
   - ❌ *"Tailored hygiene deployment"* ➔ ✅ *"We clean the areas you choose"*

5. **Keep Brand Identity and Essential Trust Terms**
   - Keep our brand name **Garuda Cleaning Services** clearly stated in key titles and intro sections.
   - Everyday professional trust words like **"Professional"** and **"Deep Cleaning"** are welcome and expected because customers look for them.
   - The goal is to remove dense, convoluted, or unnatural jargon—not essential brand words.

6. **Headings Should Sound Human and Clear**
   - Write titles like something a person would say in conversation, not an advertisement slogan.
   - ❌ *"Revolutionizing Urban Living Spaces with Mechanized Hygiene"*
   - ✅ *"Garuda Cleaning Services — Professional Cleaning in Tirupati"*

---

## 2. Locality Guidelines

- Mention localities naturally and avoid repetitive keyword lists.
- Mention key areas once on the page in a natural sentence: *"We serve homes and offices across Tirupati, including Balaji Colony, MR Palli, and nearby areas."*
- For full coverage details, link cleanly to `/service-areas` (*"See all areas we cover"*).
- Only reference genuine Tirupati localities defined in `BUSINESS_CONFIG.serviceAreas`.

---

## 3. Operational Honesty

- Never claim unverified response times (e.g. *"20-minute arrival"* or *"within 2 hours"*).
- State real operational hours clearly: *"Monday to Sunday, 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM."*
- If a customer-facing procedure (like a joint final walkthrough) is pending business owner confirmation, mark it in code with `// TODO_OWNER: confirm procedure`.
